Inspired byCursor Compile (name drawn as looping one-line curves), Craft, Linear's restraint

Palette
MotionCurves draw one after another with the app's draw, hold and erase timing
Craft · calm type over soft colour Linear · precision and restraint Highlight · quiet light grey

Palette

Craft · painted sky, app below Arc / Dia · soft glow, serif Cluely · serif on a blue sky

Also study Seasats (8.4) for dusk-lit calm.

Crate

Digging through a crate of records. Sleeves coloured by key flip forward one by one, each showing its analysis, while the vinyl slides out with the logo drawing on its label at 33⅓ rpm. Warm, physical and playful.

Inspired byThird Man Records, Mosby's Files (Awwwards, 8.0, flip-through folders), Potion
TypeBricolage Grotesque 800, Inter for text
Palette
MotionSleeves flip through on their own, vinyl spins at 33⅓, click a sleeve to pull it
Third Man Records · sleeves and vinyl Daptone · the cover as hero Potion · bold, colourful objects

Also study Mosby's Files (8.0) for flipping through a stack.

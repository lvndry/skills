## Type

- [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com): the only host claude.ai Artifacts allow. Strong, less overused picks: Instrument Sans (has a width axis) and Instrument Serif, Inter Tight, Archivo (width 62 to 125), Bricolage Grotesque, Schibsted Grotesk, Chivo, Hanken Grotesk, Familjen Grotesk, Newsreader, Fraunces, Young Serif, Big Shoulders Display, Anton.
- [Fontshare](https://www.fontshare.com), [Pangram Pangram](https://pangrampangram.com), [Collletttivo](https://www.collletttivo.it), [Uncut](https://uncut.wtf): free display faces. Self-host or inline them as data URIs; their CDNs are blocked in Artifacts.
- [Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com), [Typewolf](https://www.typewolf.com): see faces in real work and find pairings.
- [Type Scale](https://type-scale.com): build a scale.
